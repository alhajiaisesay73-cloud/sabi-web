WASSCE English synonyms: practice questions with answers

In WASSCE English Paper 1, a synonym question gives you a sentence with one word underlined. You choose the option nearest in meaning to that word. Here are 10 practice questions with answers. Tap an option to check yourself.

How to answer it

Do the swap test
Put each option in place of the word in capitals. Read the sentence again. The right answer keeps the meaning the same.Mariama is a BRIGHT pupil = Mariama is a CLEVER pupil.
Watch for look-alike words
Some options look or sound like the word but mean something else. Check the meaning, not the spelling.QUIET and "quite" look close, but they are different words.
Think about this sentence
Many words have more than one meaning. Pick the meaning that fits this sentence, not the first meaning you know.A BRIGHT pupil is clever. A bright light is shining.

Question 1

Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word in capitals:

There is one VACANT seat at the back of the keke.

See the answer

Answer: D. empty
“Vacant” means “empty”, with nobody sitting in it.

Question 2

Choose the option nearest in meaning to the word in capitals:

When the rain began, the traders HASTENED to cover their goods.

See the answer

Answer: B. hurried
“Hastened” means “hurried”. The traders moved fast to cover their goods.

Question 3

See the answer

Answer: D. throw away
“Discard” means “throw away” because it is not wanted.

Questions students ask

What is a synonym?

A synonym is a word that means the same, or nearly the same, as another word. "Big" is a synonym of "large".

How do I answer synonym questions in WASSCE English?

Put each option in place of the underlined word and read the sentence again. The option that keeps the meaning the same is the answer.

How can I learn more synonyms?

Read something in English every day and write new words in a notebook with their meaning. WAEC's Chief Examiner's report for 2023 also gives wide reading as the way to build vocabulary.
