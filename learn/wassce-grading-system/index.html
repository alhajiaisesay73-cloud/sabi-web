WASSCE grading system: A1 to F9 explained

WASSCE results use nine grades: A1, B2, B3, C4, C5, C6, D7, E8 and F9. A1 is the best. A1 to C6 are credits, D7 and E8 are passes, and F9 is a fail.

Updated 22 October 2026 by the Sabi team.

What are the WASSCE grades and marks?

The nine grades, with the mark ranges most often shown, are below. WAEC does not publish these ranges as a fixed table for every exam, so treat the marks as a guide.

GradeMeaningMarks often shown
A1Excellent75 to 100
B2Very good70 to 74
B3Good65 to 69
C4Credit60 to 64
C5Credit55 to 59
C6Credit50 to 54
D7Pass45 to 49
E8Pass40 to 44
F9Fail0 to 39

What does C6 mean in WASSCE?

C6 is the lowest credit. It is often shown as 50 to 54 marks. A C6 still counts as a credit for university.

What is the difference between a pass and a credit?

A credit is A1 to C6. A pass is D7 or E8. Universities and many jobs ask for credits, so a pass is usually not enough.

What does F9 mean in WASSCE?

F9 is a fail. It is the lowest grade, often shown as below 40 marks.

Which grade do I need in English?

Aim for at least C6, which is a credit. Universities in Sierra Leone ask for a credit in English Language.
