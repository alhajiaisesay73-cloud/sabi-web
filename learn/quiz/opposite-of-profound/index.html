Answer: A. consistent

Question 2

Choose the option most nearly opposite in meaning to the word in capitals that also fills the gap:

The first question was DIFFICULT, but the second one was .

See the answer

Answer: B. easy
“Difficult” means hard to do. “Easy” means not hard. “Easily” is an adverb, so it does not fit after “was”.
