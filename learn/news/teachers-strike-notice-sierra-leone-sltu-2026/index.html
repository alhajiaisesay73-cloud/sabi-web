Teachers' union gives a 21-day strike notice in Sierra Leone: what it means for pupils

Published 24 November 2026 by the Sabi team.

The Sierra Leone Teachers' Union (SLTU) has rejected the government's pay offer and given a 21-day strike notice that starts on Wednesday 25 November 2026, three outlets reported on 24 November 2026. This is a notice, not a strike. Teachers have not been told to stop work, and the union has asked them to stay calm while talks go on.

Are teachers on strike in Sierra Leone now?

No. The union has given the government 21 days to make a better offer. A strike can follow if the two sides do not agree before the notice ends.

When could a teachers' strike start?

The reports do not give a start date for a strike. The notice starts on 25 November 2026 and lasts 21 days, so it runs to about the middle of December 2026. The reports do not say if weekends count.

What pay offer did the teachers reject?

The government offered three increases, which add up to 50% over three years. The union says this is not enough because of the high cost of living.

WhenIncrease offered
January 202720%
January 202815%
January 202915%

What does the teachers' union want?

Two outlets report that SLTU wants salaries harmonised, not only a percentage increase. One of them says this is because teachers with different qualifications can end up on the same pay.

How did the talks get here?

The union gave the government a draft Collective Bargaining Agreement on 21 September 2026. The parts that are not about salary are already agreed. The two sides met on 20 November 2026 to finish the salary part, but they did not agree.

What has the government said about the strike notice?

None of the reports we read carry a reply from the government yet. We will update this page when there is one.

Should I still go to school?

Yes. The reports do not say that any school is closing. Go to school as usual, and wait for your school, SLTU or the Ministry of Basic and Senior Secondary Education to announce any change.
